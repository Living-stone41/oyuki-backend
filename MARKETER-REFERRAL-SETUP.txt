OYUKI MARKETER REFERRAL UPDATE

BUSINESS RULES
- Admin creates MARKETER accounts from the admin dashboard.
- An OTP is sent to the marketer's registered email.
- The marketer activates at /marketer-activate.html and creates a password.
- Normal users earn NGN 200 for each OTP-verified referral.
- Marketers earn NGN 2,000 only when the OTP-verified referred role is SELLER or KITCHEN.
- Marketers do not earn from CUSTOMER or RIDER sign-ups.
- Withdrawal is blocked until 20 qualified referrals are reached.

RAILWAY VARIABLES
REFERRAL_NORMAL_REWARD=200
REFERRAL_MARKETER_REWARD=2000
REFERRAL_REFERRED_USER_REWARD=0
REFERRAL_MINIMUM_WITHDRAWAL_REFERRALS=20

MYSQL
If users.role is a MySQL ENUM, run the marketer ALTER TABLE statement in DATABASE-UPDATES.sql once.
